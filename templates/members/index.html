DBMS Viva Note: Relational Integrity & Queries

This page executes a multi-table SQL query: SELECT m.*, p.plan_name, t.name AS trainer_name FROM members m LEFT JOIN plans p ON m.plan_id = p.plan_id LEFT JOIN trainers t ON m.trainer_id = t.trainer_id. The LEFT JOIN guarantees that members are displayed even if their plan or trainer is NULL. Email is protected by a UNIQUE constraint, and operations are protected against SQL injection using parameterized statements.
