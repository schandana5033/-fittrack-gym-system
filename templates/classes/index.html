DBMS Viva Note: Foreign Key Relationship & Schedule Management

Classes are modeled as an entity linked to the trainers table: SELECT c.*, t.name AS trainer_name, t.specialization FROM classes c LEFT JOIN trainers t ON c.trainer_id = t.trainer_id ORDER BY c.class_id ASC. This illustrates a One-to-Many relationship: one trainer can lead multiple group classes.
