DBMS Viva Note: Entity Definition & Aggregations

The plans table defines membership packages with durations and pricing. The enrolled member count is computed using an aggregate query: SELECT p.*, COUNT(m.member_id) AS enrolled_count FROM plans p LEFT JOIN members m ON p.plan_id = m.plan_id GROUP BY p.plan_id.
